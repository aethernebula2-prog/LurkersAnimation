Selected Part Properties

Position X/Y support two decimal places. Set Pivot X/Y independently. For the body, position is its top-left corner; for limbs, it is the default top-edge center.

Animation Parameters

Amplitude controls swing range; frequency controls cycles per second; phase offsets motion relative to other parts.

Editing Tips

Coordinates are measured in grid cells. Position and pivot values support two decimal places. Each part has independent Pivot X/Y; limbs default to the top-edge center and the body to its top-left corner. The body does not swing. Positive angles rotate clockwise. Face Left mirrors the full composition.

GIF export generates frames across the loop duration. Limb motion is calculated from the current parameters.
